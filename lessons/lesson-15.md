# Lesson 15

## Icelandic sound effects

The first three are more casual and used for conversation. The last one is more appropriate for giving speeches.

* oj - ew or yuck
* obbosi - oops
* jebbsi Pepsi - yup or yeah
* úps - oops
* jebb - yep
* jebbs - yep
* jubb - yup
* jöbb - yup
* jamm - yup
* jamms - yup

### academic pauses
* hérna - um or uh
